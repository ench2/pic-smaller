隐私政策 / Privacy policy

更新日期 / Updated: 2026-09-19

图片处理

图片在浏览器内处理。本工具不会把图片内容、文件名或文件路径发送给 Google Analytics，也不会为了压缩而把图片上传到我们的服务器。

可选访问统计

只有在你选择“允许统计”后，网站才会加载 Google Analytics 4。它使用 Cookie（例如 _ga）来衡量访问及会话，并接收页面路径、来源网站、设备和浏览器信息，以及图片导入数量、压缩结果、格式和下载操作。连接 Google 服务会使 Google 接收到网络请求信息（包括 IP 地址）；GA4 声明不会记录或存储个体 IP 地址。统计事件不包含图片内容、文件名、原始错误消息、页面查询参数或片段。

用途是了解网站使用情况、改进工具。Google 可能在其他国家或地区处理数据。本网站不启用 Google Signals 或广告个性化信号。拒绝统计不影响图片处理。

你的选择与本地存储

你可以通过工具页面左下角的“统计偏好”随时更改或撤回同意。撤回后停止后续统计并清除本网站可访问的 GA Cookie，不会撤回已经发送的数据。偏好保存于本地浏览器，有效期为 180 天；浏览器禁止存储时只对当前页面有效。语言、压缩选项也保存在本地，供工具正常使用。你可以在浏览器设置中清除这些记录。

广告

当前版本尚未加载 AdSense 广告。启用前会更新本政策，并配置适用于访客地区的广告同意管理。统计同意不等于广告个性化同意。

Image processing

Images are processed in your browser. We do not send image contents, filenames or file paths to Google Analytics, and do not upload images to our servers for compression.

Optional analytics

Google Analytics 4 loads only after you choose “Allow analytics”. It uses cookies such as _ga to measure visits and sessions, and receives page paths, referring sites, device/browser information, import counts, compression results, formats and download actions. Connections to Google expose network request information, including your IP address; GA4 states it does not log or store individual IP addresses. Our events exclude image contents, filenames, raw error messages, URL query strings and fragments.

We use this information to understand usage and improve the tool. Google may process it in other countries. Google Signals and advertising personalization signals are not enabled by this site. Declining analytics does not affect image processing.

Your choices and local storage

Use “Analytics preferences” at the bottom left of the tool to change or withdraw consent. Withdrawal stops subsequent analytics and clears accessible GA cookies; it does not recall previously sent data. Your preference is stored in your browser for 180 days, or for the current page only if storage is blocked. Language and compression settings are also stored locally for tool functionality. You can clear these records in your browser.

Advertising

This release does not load AdSense ads. Before enabling advertising we will update this policy and configure appropriate advertising consent management. Analytics consent is not consent to personalized advertising.

See Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from partner sites.
